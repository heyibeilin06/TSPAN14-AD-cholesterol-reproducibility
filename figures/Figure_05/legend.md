Figure 5 | Cell-context and transcript-to-protein interpretation of TSPAN14 regulation. A, Evidence atlas separating the molecular phenotype and biological context of each TSPAN14 result. The canonical exon5–6 splicing quantitative trait locus (sQTL) is directly resolved in isolated microglia and observed consistently across partially overlapping GTEx neural tissues; single-nucleus expression quantitative trait loci (eQTLs) measure total TSPAN14 expression in excitatory neurons and astrocytes; adjusted Alzheimer disease (AD)-state RNA contrasts and the published microglial perturbation constitute separate evidence layers. Blank cells indicate combinations not resolved by the available data. B, Risk-allele-aligned effects of rs7080009 on total TSPAN14 expression in astrocyte and excitatory-neuron nuclei across available ROSMAP analyses. Points denote effect estimates and horizontal lines denote 95% confidence intervals. These eQTLs provide cell-resolved regulatory context but do not measure the exon5-6 event. C, TSPAN14 AD-state RNA effects from covariate-adjusted SEA-AD donor-level pseudobulk models and an independent 17-study single-cell meta-analysis. Horizontal intervals are shown where model-based standard errors were available. All displayed estimates remained above the 5% false-discovery threshold, separating disease-state abundance from genotype-dependent regulation. D, TSPAN14-207 exons 5-7 aligned to residue-level AlphaFold confidence and reference secondary-structure assignments across the annotated EC2/ADAM10-interaction region. The canonical exon5–6 coding boundary maps to AA150/151 and has a mean boundary pLDDT of 93.3; this localizes the reference boundary but does not predict a cryptic product. The dashed horizontal line marks pLDDT = 90. E, Schematic localization of AA150/151 within the extracellular EC2 loop of the four-pass TSPAN14 topology. The inset summarizes reference-model confidence at the splice boundary; topology positions are schematic and not residue-scaled.
