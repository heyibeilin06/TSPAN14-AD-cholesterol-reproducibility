Figure 3 | Cross-tissue consistency and count-level magnitude of TSPAN14 canonical–cryptic acceptor choice. A, Canonical exon5–6 splicing quantitative trait locus (sQTL) associations for six risk-aligned variants across anterior cingulate cortex BA24, hippocampus, putamen and cervical spinal cord. Circle size represents association strength and color represents normalized effect size; bracketed alleles are aligned to the Alzheimer disease-risk haplotype. B, Left, donor-level canonical read fraction within the local two-acceptor cluster in 147 GTEx BA24 donors stratified by rs7080009 AD-risk C-allele dosage. Individual observations and distribution summaries show the complete data; all 106 C/C donors had a canonical fraction of 100%. Right, count-model and read-depth sensitivity for cryptic-acceptor use. The local fraction is not conventional exon-inclusion percent spliced in. C, Left, hexagonal density plot of exon5–6 and exon6–7 junction counts across 2,642 GTEx brain samples. Right, Spearman correlations across 13 brain regions; point size represents sample number. D, Coordinate-accurate splice graph for ENST00000429989 showing the canonical exon5–6 event, competing cryptic acceptor, adjacent exon6–7 junction and rs7080009. GRCh38 coordinates are shown.
