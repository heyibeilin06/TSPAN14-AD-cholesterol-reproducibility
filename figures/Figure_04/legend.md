Figure 4 | Locus-level directional sensitivity and causal scope at the TSPAN14 locus. A, Risk-aligned association Z scores for five correlated, genome-wide significant cis-splicing quantitative trait locus (cis-sQTL) variants tagging one haplotype across the canonical TSPAN14 exon5–6 branch, Alzheimer disease (AD), total cholesterol (TC), low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and non-high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (non-HDL-C). Circle area and color intensity encode the absolute Z score; all effects are aligned to the inferred AD-risk-increasing allele. B, Effect estimates from linkage-disequilibrium-aware generalized inverse-variance-weighted (IVW) models using the rebuilt 1000 Genomes European LD matrix and lead-variant Wald ratios. Points denote estimates and horizontal lines denote 95% confidence intervals. The AD and lipid estimates are displayed on separate x-axis scales because their effect units differ. C, Diagnostic summary for the four local outcomes. Circle size indicates the strength of evidence against the prespecified diagnostic threshold; red marks indicate threshold failures. non-HDL-C showed evidence of heterogeneity and a non-null Mendelian-randomization (MR)-Egger intercept. D, Genome-wide univariable MR estimates for five lipid traits on AD before and after exclusion of an extended APOE-region interval. Points denote inverse-variance-weighted estimates and horizontal lines denote 95% confidence intervals. E, Direct effects from a jointly clumped genome-wide multivariable MR model for LDL-C, HDL-C and triglycerides (left), together with conditional F statistics across assumed pairwise sampling-error correlations (right). The horizontal dashed line marks F = 10. F, Principal-component generalized method-of-moments sensitivity analysis across retained linkage-disequilibrium dimensions. The left panel shows attenuation of each lipid coefficient after adjustment for exon5–6 usage; the right panel shows the corresponding minimum conditional F statistic. Filled points indicate dimensions for which both conditional F statistics were at least 10; no tested dimension satisfied the prespecified confirmatory mediation criteria. Numerical estimates and diagnostic definitions are provided in the corresponding source-data tables.
