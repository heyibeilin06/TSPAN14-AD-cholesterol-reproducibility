Figure 2 | Variant-level resolution of the TSPAN14 regulatory locus. A, Regional association statistics for Alzheimer disease (AD), total cholesterol, low-density lipoprotein cholesterol, non-high-density lipoprotein cholesterol and the canonical BA24 TSPAN14 exon5–6 splicing quantitative trait locus (sQTL), aligned on the same GRCh38 coordinate. Variants are colored by European-ancestry linkage disequilibrium (LD; r2) with rs7080009; the open circles identify rs7080009 in each track. B, GENCODE v38 TSPAN14 transcript models, with ENST00000429989 highlighted, together with the canonical exon5–6 junction, ENCODE candidate cis-regulatory elements and published CRISPR interference or prime-editing anchors. Project proxy variants are shown on the same coordinate. C, Locus-comparison plots relating the canonical exon5–6 sQTL association strength to AD and lipid association strength. High-LD variants are colored as in A, and canonical-branch colocalization posterior probabilities (PP.H4) are shown. D, Compact annotation matrix for sentinel and proxy variants. Circle size and opacity encode evidence magnitude; colors distinguish LD, project annotation, external sQTL replication and published perturbation or editing evidence. CS, credible set.
