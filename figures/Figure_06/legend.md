Blue lines denote present-study associations; teal, tissue or cell context; purple dotted, reference localization; orange dashed, published perturbation; and grey dashed, predictions requiring validation. Published rs7922621 editing linked the block to TSPAN14 regulation, cell-surface ADAM10 and soluble TREM2 without targeting either splice acceptor. Parallel AD and cholesterol-trait inputs denote shared local association, not serial lipid-to-splicing-to-AD mediation.
