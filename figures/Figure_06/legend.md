Figure 6 | Integrated interpretation of the TSPAN14 regulatory-splicing locus. Alzheimer disease (AD), total cholesterol (TC), low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and non-high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (non-HDL-C) show a shared local association configuration across a high-linkage-disequilibrium regulatory block. The splice readout is represented as risk-haplotype-dependent balance between the canonical exon5–6 acceptor and the previously characterized competing cryptic acceptor. Blue solid lines denote associations analyzed in the present study; teal lines denote cross-tissue or cell-context observations; orange dashed lines denote published regulatory perturbation evidence; grey dashed lines denote splice-specific predictions requiring experimental validation. The published rs7922621 editing result connects the regulatory block with TSPAN14 expression, cell-surface ADAM10 and soluble TREM2 but does not perturb the splice acceptors directly. AA150/151 localizes the canonical boundary within the extracellular EC2 region. The parallel AD and cholesterol-trait inputs indicate a shared local configuration and do not specify a serial lipid-to-splicing-to-AD mediation pathway.
